The mission

A quick arcade challenge built around movement, positioning, timing, and survival—not graphic combat.

01 / DEFEND

Guard the village

Navigate the dusty lanes and fields of a fictional Punjab pind while five raiders close in.

02 / STRIKE

Choose your moment

Face a nearby raider and time your sword strike. Attacks only connect within reach and in front of you.

03 / SURVIVE

Finish the level

Defeat all five raiders before the timer expires, while protecting your three health points.

Behind the build

A small project showing how an AI-assisted development workflow can turn a game idea into a playable web experience.

Design goals

  • Keep the goal clear: defeat five enemies.
  • Provide immediate feedback through score, health, timer, and attack effects.
  • Support desktop keyboards and touch controls.
  • Use a custom-drawn setting inspired by rural Punjab.

Tools & limitations

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the Canvas API. The prototype runs in the browser without a server or API key. The game has one level and uses simple shapes for its characters and environment.

AI disclosure: AI assistance was used to support development and documentation. This site has no live AI chatbot or online database.

The warrior and conflict are fictional and not intended to depict a specific historical or religious event.
